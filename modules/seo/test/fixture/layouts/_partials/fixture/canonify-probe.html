{{/* Publishes the OPERANDS of the canonifyURLs mechanism, so the build that turns the setting on can prove it took effect and a future Hugo change to the mechanism names itself instead of quietly making that build's assertions vacuous. The module itself emits only absolute URLs, so nothing it publishes changes between the two builds -- which is the result being asserted, and exactly why the assertion needs a subject of its own. Two spellings of one value, chosen for how Hugo's post-processor treats the attribute NAME (measured at v0.164.0): it rewrites root-relative URLs in attributes ending in href, src, srcset, action and url, and leaves every other attribute alone. - data-canonify-raw shows what the template produced. With the setting off it carries the baseURL path; with it on, relURL drops the path. - data-canonify-href shows what a reader receives. With the setting on the post-processor absolutizes it back onto the full baseURL, which is why a relURL-derived URL is safe in HTML and unsafe everywhere else. */}}
