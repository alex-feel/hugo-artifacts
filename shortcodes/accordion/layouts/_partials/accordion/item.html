Deliberate contract points: - Zero ARIA.
/ carries the expand/collapse state natively (WAI-ARIA 1.2 section 1.1's first rule of ARIA: use the host language feature); adding aria-expanded, aria-controls, or role="button" to a summary is redundant at best and measurably harmful at worst (it can suppress state announcements and hide a nested heading from VoiceOver). Anchoring the body is what makes a deep link to a closed item actually reveal it where the platform supports the algorithm. - The heading, when requested, wraps ONLY the title span; the icon stays outside it so heading navigation announces the title text alone.
